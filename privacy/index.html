Privacy Policy for Close 1

Last updated: October 16, 2026

Close 1 is a live sports scores app for iOS. It shows scores and standings and can send you notifications when a game is close or goes to overtime. This policy explains what data Close 1 handles and how.

Data stored on your device

Close 1 stores your settings on your device, such as your favorite teams, your notification choices, and which tabs you show. This data stays on your device.

Data sent to our server

To send you notifications, Close 1 sends the following to a server we run: your device's push notification address (a token provided by Apple), whether notifications are on, which leagues and favorite teams you want notifications for, and any choices you make for individual games. We use this information only to decide which notifications to send you and to send them. It is not linked to your name, email address, or any account, because Close 1 has no accounts.

Data we do not collect

Close 1 does not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, or any other personal information. It does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising services, and it does not sell your data.

Third parties

  • Apple delivers notifications through the Apple Push Notification service.
  • Cloudflare hosts our server.
  • ESPN provides scores, standings, team information, and logos. Your device requests this directly from ESPN, so ESPN may see standard internet information such as your IP address. Close 1 sends ESPN no personal information.

We do not share your data with anyone else.

Your choices

You can turn notifications off in Close 1 or in your iPhone's Settings at any time. To ask us to delete the information associated with your device, email us at the address below.

Trademarks

Team, league, and TV provider names and logos belong to their owners. Close 1 is not affiliated with or endorsed by any league, team, TV provider, or ESPN.

Changes to this policy

If how Close 1 handles data changes, we will update this policy.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email zach@zkeldesign.com with your device, iOS version and what you saw. In the app, Settings > About > Send Feedback opens a pre-addressed email.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a close game?

With five minutes left, or in overtime, a game is close when it is within 5 points in the NBA and men's college basketball, within 8 points in the NFL and college football, or within 1 goal in the NHL. Once a game qualifies as close, it stays close until it ends.

Which leagues are supported?

NFL, FBS college football, NBA, NHL and men's college basketball (Division I). In Settings, Leagues lets you turn leagues off and reorder them.

Why didn't I get a notification?

Check three things. Notifications must be allowed for Close 1 in iPhone Settings. The league or team must be turned on in Close 1's Settings. And the game must have met the close-game rule above. Games that are already final don't send notifications. Notifications may arrive a minute or so after a game becomes close.

How do I choose which games notify me?

In Settings, open Notifications. Turn on whole leagues or your My Teams. You can also override any single game from its game details screen.

How do I cut down on college notifications?

In Settings, open Notifications and choose the college football or men's college basketball page. You can pick a preset such as Top 25 or toggle individual conferences. Your My Teams still notify you.

How fresh are the scores?

Scores come from ESPN and refresh whenever you open the app or return to it. Open game screens refresh on their own while the game is live.

Is Close 1 free?

Yes. Close 1 is free to use. It doesn't show ads or promote betting.

Does it have widgets and light mode?

Yes to both. The widgets follow your Home Screen appearance, and the app has System, Light and Dark appearance settings.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

Where does the data come from?

Game data and logos are provided by ESPN. Close 1 is not affiliated with or endorsed by ESPN, any league or team, or any TV provider.

How do I delete my data?

Close 1 has no accounts. To ask us to delete the information associated with your device, email zach@zkeldesign.com. See the Privacy Policy for what Close 1 handles.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email zach@zkeldesign.com with your device, iOS version and what you saw. In the app, Settings > About > Send Feedback opens a pre-addressed email.

Frequently asked questions

What counts as a close game?

In the last five minutes, a game is close when it's within 5 points in the NBA and men's college basketball, 8 points in the NFL and college football, or 1 goal in the NHL. Every overtime game counts as close. Once a game is close, it stays close until it ends.

Which leagues are supported?

NFL, NBA, NHL, FBS college football and men's college basketball (Division I). To turn leagues off or reorder them, go to Settings > Leagues.

Why didn't I get a notification?

Check three things. Notifications must be allowed for Close 1 in iPhone Settings. The league or team must be turned on in Close 1's Settings. And the game must have met the close-game rule above. Final games don't send notifications, and alerts can lag a minute or so behind the game.

How do I choose which games notify me?

Go to Settings > Notifications. Turn on whole leagues or your My Teams. To override a single game, open its game details screen.

How do I cut down on college notifications?

Go to Settings > Notifications and open the college football or men's college basketball page. Pick a preset like Top 25 or toggle individual conferences. Your My Teams still notify you.

How fresh are the scores?

Scores come from ESPN and refresh whenever you open the app or return to it. Open game screens update on their own while the game is live.

Is Close 1 free?

Yes. Free, with no ads and no betting promotion. If you want to support it, there's an optional tip jar (opens in a new tab).

Do tips unlock anything?

No. Tips are optional and don't unlock features. Close 1 works the same whether you tip or not.

Where does my tip go?

Tips go toward servers and development. They're handled by Buy Me a Coffee, and Close 1 never sees your payment details.

Does it have widgets and light mode?

Yes to both. Widgets follow your Home Screen appearance. The app has System, Light and Dark settings.

What do I need to run it?

An iPhone running iOS 26 or later.

Where does the data come from?

Game data and logos are provided by ESPN. Close 1 is not affiliated with or endorsed by ESPN, any league or team, or any TV provider.

How do I delete my data?

Close 1 has no accounts. To ask us to delete the information associated with your device, email zach@zkeldesign.com. The Privacy Policy covers what Close 1 handles.
